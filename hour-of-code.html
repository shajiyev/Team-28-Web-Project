The Hour of Code

The Mission and First Step

In the Hour of Code, we had to teach coding to 20 children who didn't have any information about it. Our first mission was to find 20 participants. Instructors advised us to find students from the neighborhood, but we could not find them. That is why we went to a course which included 5–9 class students who study in the Azerbaijani language, so they did not know anything about our topic. After finding the children, we had to provide them with the needed equipment, so we went to the school where Davud previously studied.

The Second Step

In class, we first explained our aim to the children, then Sabir and Davud started the presentation about ADA University and persuaded the children to come to ADA. Their eyes were full of enthusiasm and with every new piece of information about the university they were getting more involved.

The Third Step

Davud provided the basic information about coding to the participants. Then the whole class started working.

The Fourth Step

Sabir, Aziz, and Samira worked with the children individually and Davud worked with the rest of them, because there were 20 computers for 26 participants.

Problems

  • Certificates: We had 20 certificates, but then we learned that there were 26 children. After the lesson, we printed 6 more certificates and presented them to the rest of the participants.

  • Children's noise: They could not adapt to the lesson and were making a lot of noise, but then we managed it all by telling them that this way we would not be able to teach them anything.
